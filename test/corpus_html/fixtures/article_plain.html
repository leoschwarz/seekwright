Harbour ferry timetable changes on Monday

The winter timetable starts a week early this year, the harbour master said, because the autumn school holidays moved.

First departure from the east pier stays at 06:12, and the last crossing back leaves the west pier at 22:40 until the end of February.

Weekend frequencies drop from every fifteen minutes to every half hour, and the late Saturday boat is withdrawn for the winter.

Season ticket holders are unaffected. The harbour office will reprint the printed timetable in the second week of November.
