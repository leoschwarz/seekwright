Knots, in the order the examiner asks

Bowline
A fixed loop that will not slip under load and can still be untied after it has been loaded.
Clove hitch
Two half hitches on the bight; the knot a deckhand reaches for first and the one that capsizes first.
Sheet bend
Joins two lines of different diameters, which is the case a reef knot gets wrong.

The three the examiner calls

  1. Bowline
  2. Round turn and two half hitches
    • the turn takes the load
    • the half hitches take the turn
  3. Sheet bend
